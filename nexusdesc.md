[size=6][b]What Does This Mod Do?[/b][/size]
Sell Me Slave! (SMS) adds a brand new dynamic to Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord: [b]The Underground Prisoner Trade[/b]. You can now visit the backstreets of any town to find the "Captive Trading" area or interact with Lords directly on the map to buy their prisoners. Through a seamless, native-like trade interface, you can purchase regular prisoner troops to bolster your ranks or even buy out high-value captive Heroes from across Calradia.

[line]

[size=6][b]Key Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Native Trade UI Integration:[/b] Seamlessly buy prisoners using the native party screen with dynamic bottom-panel cost calculations.
[*][b]Captive Trading (Towns):[/b] Visit town backstreets to access randomized prisoner pools. Lower-tier troops are common, while elite units are rare.
[*][b]Buy Captive Heroes:[/b] Buy captive Lords from across the realm. They are not teleported; they will be physically delivered to your party based on distance!
[*][b]Map Encounters:[/b] Illegal trading isn't limited to towns. Talk to any Lord on the map to negotiate for their prisoners.
[*][b]Moral & Social Consequences:[/b] Dealing in slaves grants [b]Roguery XP[/b] and increases [b]Relationship[/b] with the seller, but at a cost: your [b]Honor[/b] will suffer, and your [b]Criminal Rating[/b] will soar!
[*][b]Fully Configurable:[/b] Using the MCM menu, you can fine-tune every multiplier: Crime gain, Relation gain, Roguery XP, and Honor loss.
[*][b]Full Localization Support:[/b] Professional EN/TR text support.
[/list]

[line]

[size=6][b]Underground Consequences[/b][/size]
Human trafficking is highly illegal in Calradia (unless you're a bandit).
[list]
[*][b]Crime Rating:[/b] Buying slaves increases your criminal rating with the local faction.
[*][b]Honor Loss:[/b] Trading in human lives is dishonorable. Your Honor trait will decrease proportionally to the gold spent.
[*][b]Roguery XP:[/b] You gain Serserilik (Roguery) experience for every successful illegal transaction, with real-time UI notifications.
[*][b]Social Gains:[/b] Illegal gold is still gold! Lords will like you more for providing them with "off-the-record" funding.
[/list]

[size=6][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Q: How do I access the trader?[/b]
A: In towns, go to "Go to the backstreet" and select [b]"Captive Trading"[/b]. On the map, talk to a Lord and look for the new trade option.
[b]Q: Why don't I see certain Lords in the buy list?[/b]
A: The mod correctly filters out Heroes held by your own party or clan to prevent "buying from yourself".
[b]Q: Is it safe to add mid-save?[/b]
A: Yes. It is perfectly safe to add to an existing campaign.
